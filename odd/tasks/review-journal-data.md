S3: Initial inconsistencies: a nonempty unparseable play date; a finish date earlier than its start; numeric review/hour values outside supported bounds in legacy data. Label ambiguous status/date combinations as suggestions rather than asserting they are invalid.
S4: Initial optional reminders: completed entries without a review, played entries without a console, and played entries without recorded hours. A review or hours value of zero counts as filled.
S5: Missing exact dates or approximate periods for Recuerdo are not errors. Missing Steam/Nintendo pages and links to other legitimate sites are not data-quality errors.
S6: Each item explains why it appears and offers Ver juego / Editar. Never fabricate dates, scores, consoles, or comments, and never bulk-correct or merge entries automatically.
S7: Let users enable optional reminder types, hide an individual finding, and show hidden findings again. Hide only that specific finding; if its relevant underlying values change, evaluate it again. Do not use the entire entry revision as the fingerprint, since unrelated edits should not revive a dismissed reminder.
S8: Keep the checks themselves derived rather than storing copies of games or lists of findings.
S9: Persist account-level reminder preferences and owner-scoped dismissals so they work across devices. This is the only planned database addition.
S10: Use an additive migration with explicit grants/RLS, validated API boundaries, and owner checks for referenced entries. Avoid browser access to any other account's records or settings.
S11: Extend versioned complete snapshots and recovery validation for these settings, preserving compatibility with older backup formats. Restore must preserve explicit owner mapping. Keep existing game-export columns unchanged.
S12: Review the actual migration and updated backup reader together before requesting SQL approval; do not publish a client that requires an unapplied migration.
